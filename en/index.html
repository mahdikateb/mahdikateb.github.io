About

Bridging Legal Expertise and Technology

I began my legal education at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad and went on to earn an LL.M. in Intellectual Property Law from Shahid Beheshti University in Tehran. I am currently pursuing a PhD in Private Law at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad while practicing as a trainee attorney at the Khorasan Bar Association.

During my collaboration with the National Elites Foundation, I conducted research on the legal protection of the “Tayyeb” certification trademark and served as an intellectual property advisor to the Razavi Quality Institute. These experiences, together with my academic research, have shaped my approach to the legal challenges of businesses and emerging technologies.
